How It All Began

Finelo was made from a single bold idea:

We make financial education accessible, actionable and—actually fun. Because your future shouldn't be confusing or out of reach.

We started Finelo because too many people want to take control of their finances but feel stuck at square one: "What's a dividend?" "How do I start trading?" "Will I lose everything?" Rather than joining the usual chaos of forums, confusing blogs, or vague YouTube clips, our founders asked:

So we gathered the best minds across tech, finance and education sectors to construct accredited tools and support frameworks for learning. We built bite-sized lessons, simulations and AI-support to guide you. From the commitment to deliver accessible finance knowledge— Finelo was born.

What We're Aiming For

To become the #1 trusted platform for financial growth, empowering millions to learn smarter, invest wiser, and own their financial future with confidence.

We envision a world where anyone — no matter their background — can understand money, make smart investment decisions, and grow their wealth without fear. Not just with tips or hacks, but with real learning, hands-on tools, and supportive guidance.

Is Finelo a Brokerage App?

No. Finelo is an educational product, not a brokerage.

In Demo mode, you practice with virtual funds without placing real trades or connecting a brokerage account. Brokerage connection availability depends on your region.

Finelo's job ends where your real account begins. The goal is that you have the frameworks, the practice, and the confidence to act on your own terms.

  • Bite-sized daily lessons, quizzes, and real-world scenarios.
  • Simulator practice with real market data and virtual funds.
  • AI Chart Analyzer, Smart Pattern Finder, AI Mentor, and educational AI agent demonstrations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Finelo?

It's a beginner-friendly learning environment for trading and investing. Inside, you'll find 28-day challenges, bite-sized daily lessons, a simulator with real market data and virtual funds, AI tools for market analysis, and a habit layer built around streaks and consistency.

Is Finelo a trading app or a learning app?

Finelo is a learning app first. It teaches you how trading and investing work and gives you a simulator to practice decisions safely.

Is Finelo a brokerage app?

No. Finelo is an educational product, not a brokerage.

Does the Finelo app use real money?

Demo mode uses virtual funds and real market data. Brokerage connection availability depends on your region.

What is Finelo AI?

The AI-assisted learning track inside Finelo. It includes two 28-day challenges (AI Trader and AI Investing), three AI tools for market analysis, and three AI agents that demonstrate different agent strategies on real market data.

Is there a Finelo community I can join?
