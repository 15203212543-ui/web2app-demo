Frequently Asked Questions

What is Finelo’s AI Trading Bot?

Finelo’s AI trading bots identify potential trade setups for you to review. You decide whether to approve a proposed trade.

Do Finelo’s AI bots trade without my approval?

You approve each proposed trade before it opens. Once a trade is open, it can close automatically.

Is Finelo’s AI bot worth it?

Consider whether you want structured learning, virtual practice, and trade setups you review yourself. Compare the included features and renewal cost with your needs. The reviews on this page describe individual early learning experiences.

How much does AI bot access cost?

Check the current offer for the price, renewal terms, and included bot features. Payment for Finelo access is separate from money used for trading. View pricing information.

Can an AI trading bot guarantee a profit?

No. Real-money trading involves risk of loss. Demo practice and customer reviews do not establish future trading results.
