Frequently asked questions

How is the required monthly contribution calculated?

The calculator compounds current savings monthly, then works backward to find the monthly amount needed to cover the gap.

What if I already have enough saved to reach my goal?

The required monthly contribution is zero if the projected value of your current savings meets your target.

Should I use a conservative or optimistic interest rate?

Compare several assumptions to see how the required monthly contribution changes.

Does this account for inflation?

No. Amounts are shown in nominal dollars.
