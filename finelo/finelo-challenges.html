Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Finelo 28-day challenge?

A daily learning plan that combines short lessons, quizzes and practice over four weeks.

What is Finelo, and who is it built for?

Finelo is an educational platform for learning how markets, trading and investing work, including for beginners.

Can I start with as little as $20?

The original offer advertises a first-month discount price of $19.99 for its four-week plan. Simulator practice uses virtual funds.

Is Finelo for trading or investing?

There are separate learning tracks for active trading and long-term investing.

Which Finelo challenge should beginners start with?

Start with the Classic track that matches your interest in trading or investing.

Does Finelo tell me what to trade?

Finelo teaches concepts and gives you a place to practice. It does not give you personalized trade instructions.

Does Finelo use real money in the simulator?

The simulator uses virtual funds and market data.

What is the difference between the Classic and AI Tracks?

The Classic track teaches the fundamentals. The AI track adds ways to use AI for research and analysis.
