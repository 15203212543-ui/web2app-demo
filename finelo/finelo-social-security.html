How we calculated this result

We apply early and delayed claiming adjustments and compare discounted monthly payments through the same assumed lifespan. The default weights monthly payments by survival using the SSA 2022 period life table. You can also compare a fixed lifespan.

Educational estimates for your own retirement benefit. This local version does not model spouse, survivor or dependent benefits, past claims, disability, work adjustments, taxes or Medicare deductions. Confirm your eligibility and official benefit with the Social Security Administration.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my full-retirement-age benefit?

Look for the full-retirement-age amount on your Social Security statement.

Does waiting until 70 always make sense?

Starting later increases the monthly amount, but also means fewer months of payments. Compare assumptions rather than relying on a single projection.

Can I use this for a married couple?

Use the original calculator for household benefit modeling.

Are the results adjusted for inflation?

Amounts are shown in today’s dollars. The discount rate is a real rate.

Why might my SSA estimate be different?

SSA uses your actual work record, eligibility and benefit rules.
