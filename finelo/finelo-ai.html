Can I Connect a Brokerage Account?

Demo mode uses virtual funds without connecting a brokerage account. Brokerage availability depends on your region.

What Every Strategy Preset Should Make Clear

What kind of investments it looks at

Stocks, ETFs, industries, or other defined assets.

How long it plans to keep an investment

Intraday, days to weeks, or long term.

Rules for when to buy and sell

Conditions that flag an idea for review.

Limits on risk

Position limits, exit rules, and other safeguards.

When not to act

Conditions where the preset should stay quiet.

Results from tests or demo practice

See how it behaved with historical data or in simulation.
