Frequently Asked Questions

What is this Finelo bank charge?

If your bank statement shows one of the names listed on this page, it is a charge from Finelo. Different banks and payment processors can display merchant names in different formats.

Why does my bank statement show a different name than "Finelo"?

Different banks and payment processors display merchant names differently. All the names listed above are variations of Finelo and refer to the same service.

I don't remember making this purchase. What should I do?
