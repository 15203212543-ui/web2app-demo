Frequently asked questions

How does compound interest work?

Interest is calculated on both your contributions and the interest already earned.

What's a realistic interest rate to use?

Compare multiple assumptions. Actual returns vary by asset and period.

Does this calculator account for taxes or fees?

No. It shows the result before fees and taxes.

How often is interest compounded in this calculator?

Monthly. The annual rate is divided by twelve, and contributions are added at the end of each month.
