Frequently asked questions

What is Coursiv?

Coursiv is a globally recognized online learning platform focused on helping people build practical digital and AI skills through short, accessible courses. Our mission is to help professionals become AI-native: confident in understanding modern AI tools and capable of applying them meaningfully in their work, communication, productivity, and daily decision-making.

Is Coursiv legit?

Yes. Coursiv is a real product operated by Coursiv Limited. You can read independent reviews on Trustpilot and the App Store, Google Play and review the legal terms on legal.coursiv.io.

Is Coursiv free?

Coursiv is a paid subscription product. You can explore the offer flow first; your subscription provides access to all courses and the full app experience.

How much does Coursiv cost?

Coursiv is a subscription product. The exact price is always shown on the checkout screen before you pay. If you have any questions, contact support@coursiv.io.

Does Coursiv have a free trial?

Promotional and introductory plans change over time. Any trial or intro pricing is shown on the payment screen before you confirm.

How do I log in to Coursiv?

Sign in at the Coursiv sign-in page on the web, or open the Coursiv app and use the same email and password. See the Login page for step-by-step help.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

On the sign-in page, use the forgot-password / reset option and follow the email instructions. If you do not receive an email, check spam or contact support@coursiv.io.

How do I cancel my Coursiv subscription?

Sign in, open your profile subscription settings on the website or in the app, and cancel before your current billing cycle ends. Full instructions are on the Cancel subscription page.

Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the Refund Policy. Cancelling stops future renewals; for a specific charge, contact support@coursiv.io.

Can I use Coursiv on both web and mobile?

Yes. One account works across the website and the iOS and Android apps with the same credentials.

Do I need experience with AI to start?

No. Coursiv is for anyone looking to use AI more effectively in their work or daily life. It covers essential tools like ChatGPT and Claude, along with specialized programs for productivity, business development, marketing, content creation, and other practical use cases. No prior technical experience is required.

Where can I read Coursiv reviews?

You can find Coursiv reviews on Trustpilot, the App Store, and Google Play, as well as browse learner feedback on our Reviews page.
