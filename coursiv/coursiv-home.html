Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about Coursiv

What is Coursiv?

Coursiv is an online educational platform that helps users learn AI tools and digital skills to advance their careers. It offers courses on in-demand skills, modern professions, and applying AI at work.

How to download and use Coursiv?

Download the Coursiv app from the Google Play Store for Android or the Apple App Store for iOS. Once installed, sign up to create an account. Then browse the available courses, choose one, and start learning at your own pace.

How to log in into Coursiv?

Open the app or visit the Coursiv website. Enter your registered email address and password in the login section. Once logged in, you can access courses, use tools, and track your progress.

How to cancel Coursiv subscription?

You can cancel your subscription in the subscription section of your account settings. Check Coursiv’s Terms and Conditions for cancellation or refund details. Contact the Support Center if you need more help.

Which payment methods are accepted?

We accept major credit and debit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. You can also pay with PayPal or through the payment systems integrated into the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Can I use my account on multiple devices?

Yes, you can log into your Coursiv account on multiple devices (desktop and mobile). Your progress is automatically synced to the cloud, allowing you to switch between your smartphone and tablet without losing your place in a lesson.

What should I do if the app is crashing?

To resolve this, please first ensure you are using the latest version of the app. If the issue persists, don't hesitate to reach out to our dedicated support team at support@coursiv.io for further assistance.

What do I get after completing a course?

After completing all modules and assessments, you’ll unlock an official digital certificate. It appears directly in your profile, and you can share it on LinkedIn or add it to your professional portfolio.
