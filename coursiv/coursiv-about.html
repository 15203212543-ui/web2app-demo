What is Coursiv?

Coursiv is a globally recognized online learning platform focused on helping people build practical digital and AI skills through short, accessible courses. Our mission is to help professionals become AI-native: confident in understanding modern AI tools and capable of applying them meaningfully in their work, communication, productivity, and daily decision-making.

What you can learn

From your very first lesson you're using AI on real tasks, not sitting through theory. By the end you can learn:

  • How to use ChatGPT, Claude, and other popular AI tools for everyday tasks
  • Prompting techniques that lead to better, more reliable results
  • How to apply AI to writing, planning, research, and content creation
  • How to use AI to boost productivity and improve workflows
  • Guided courses and challenges, including the 28-Day AI Certificate Program Challenge

How Coursiv works

  1. Take a short quiz

    You answer a few questions about your goals and experience. Coursiv uses this to build a learning plan tailored to you.

  2. Learn in short daily lessons

    Lessons are broken into bite-size steps designed to fit a busy schedule, typically a few minutes at a time.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Coursiv for?

Coursiv is for anyone looking to use AI more effectively in their work or daily life. It covers essential tools like ChatGPT and Claude, along with specialized programs for productivity, business development, marketing, content creation, and other practical use cases. No prior technical experience is required.

What will I learn on Coursiv?

You learn how to use popular AI tools in everyday tasks—writing, planning, research, and content—plus prompting techniques and ways to apply AI at work. Content is organized into short lessons and challenges like the 28-Day AI Certificate Program Challenge.

Do I need any experience to start?

No. Coursiv is easy to get started with and works for different skill levels. The onboarding quiz helps you find the right learning path, and the courses guide you step by step.
