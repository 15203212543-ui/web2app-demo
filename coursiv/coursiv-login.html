How to Log In

  1. Open the official Sign-In page

    Go to the Sign-in page on your browser, or launch the Coursiv app on your phone.

  2. Enter your email and password

    Use the email address you registered with. If you bought access through the website, that same email is your login.

  3. Continue learning

    Once you are signed in, you can open your courses, track progress, and update subscription settings from your profile.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I log in to Coursiv?

Sign in on the web at the Coursiv sign-in page, or open the Coursiv mobile app and use the same email and password.

I forgot my Coursiv password. What should I do?

On the sign-in page, use the forgot-password / reset option and follow the email instructions. If you do not receive an email, check spam or contact support@coursiv.io.

Can I use the same login on web and mobile?

Yes. Your Coursiv account works across the website and the iOS/Android apps with the same credentials.

I cannot access my account. Who can help?

Contact the Support Center or email support@coursiv.io with the email address tied to your account.
