    Frequently asked questions

    What does the prompt optimizer check?

    It checks the task, context, audience, output format, role, constraints, example, definition of done, and specific wording.

    Does it work for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini?

    Yes. These checks apply to prompts for all three tools.

    Is my prompt sent to an AI model?

    No. The checks and rewrite run in your browser.

    Why does the rewrite contain square brackets?

    They mark details to fill in or remove before using the prompt.

    How is this different from a prompt generator?

    A generator starts from scratch. The optimizer scores your existing prompt and adds slots for missing details.
