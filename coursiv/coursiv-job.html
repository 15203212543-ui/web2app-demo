      Frequently asked questions

      How is the exposure score calculated?

      AI-producible counts as 1, AI-assisted as 0.5, and person-only as 0.1. The score is their average as a percentage.

      Does a high score mean my job will disappear?

      No. It describes a mix of tasks and cannot predict job outcomes.

      Where do the ratings come from?

      The local occupation library uses sample ratings. Custom tasks are matched against browser-based rules.

      Can I use this to make decisions about my employees?

      No. The tool is for learning about categories of tasks.

      My occupation is not in the list. What do I do?

      Choose List my own tasks.
