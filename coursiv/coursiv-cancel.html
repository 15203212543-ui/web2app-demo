How to cancel your Coursiv subscription

You can cancel online anytime from your Coursiv profile on the website or in the app. Cancel before the end of your current billing cycle to avoid being charged for the next period.

Manage my subscription → Need help?Contact support → New to Coursiv?Start now →

Cancel in a few steps

  1. Sign in to your account

    Open the Coursiv sign-in page (or the Coursiv app) and sign in with the email used for your subscription.

  2. Go to subscription settings

    Open your profile, then open the subscription section where your current plan and renewal details are shown.

  3. Confirm cancellation

    Select cancel and confirm. To avoid the next charge, finish cancellation before your current billing cycle ends.

Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my Coursiv subscription?

Sign in to your Coursiv account, open your profile subscription settings on the website or in the app, and cancel from there before the end of your current billing cycle.

Will I get a refund if I cancel?

Cancellation stops future renewals according to your plan timing. Refund eligibility depends on the Refund Policy. Review Refund Policy or contact support for your specific case.

Where do I cancel if I subscribed on iOS or Android?

If you purchased through the App Store or Google Play, you may also need to manage or cancel the subscription in that store's subscription settings, in addition to checking your Coursiv account.

I canceled but was still charged. What now?

Contact support@coursiv.io or the Support Center with your account email and approximate charge date so the team can help with your case.
