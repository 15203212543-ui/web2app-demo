Frequently asked questions

Why was I charged again?

Coursiv is a subscription, so it renews automatically until you cancel. To stop future charges, cancel from your account before the next billing date.

How do I stop the charge?

Sign in to your Coursiv account, open subscription settings, and cancel before the current billing cycle ends. See the cancellation guide for step-by-step instructions.

I don't recognize this charge. What should I do?

Check the email inbox that may have received a Coursiv receipt, and confirm whether anyone with access to your card subscribed. If you still don't recognize it, contact support@coursiv.io with the approximate charge date and amount so the team can help.

Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on the Refund Policy. Contact support@coursiv.io with your account email and the charge details to review your specific case.
